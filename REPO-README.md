# ClipMaker — Paste a link. Get clips that slap.

**Live site & demo:** https://shivamkumarsehagal-web.github.io/clipmaker/
**Watch it work:** https://shivamkumarsehagal-web.github.io/clipmaker/demo.html

ClipMaker turns one long video into ranked, ready-to-post vertical clips — automatically. Paste a YouTube link and it finds the moments worth clipping, cuts them in full-frame 9:16, and burns in clean Hinglish captions placed high with a background box, at a slight speed-up for Shorts/Reels pacing.

Built for Indian creators, gaming channels, podcasters and clipping pages.

## How it works
1. **Paste a link** (or drop a video file).
2. **Signal detection** scores every moment on five axes — speech energy, audio peaks, on-screen motion, facecam reaction and chat velocity — and only keeps moments where at least 3 of 5 fire. Every clip carries its score breakdown.
3. **Smart render** — face-aware vertical crop (two-shot stacking for podcasts), punch zoom on the peak second, captions corrected and transliterated into Roman Hinglish by an AI layer, and a ready description with full source credit.

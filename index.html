01

How it works

1

Paste the link

A YouTube or Twitch VOD, a stream recording, or your own video file. We pull the video and its caption track. No captions? A built-in transcription pass drafts them.

2

Five signals hunt

Speech, audio peaks, motion, facecam reaction and chat velocity are scored separately. A moment only qualifies when at least 3 of the 5 signals fire together — single-signal guesses are how bad clips happen.

3

The AI pass

Connect your own free AI key (Groq, Gemini or OpenRouter) and an AI pass boosts hook ranking, fixes Hinglish caption mistakes and writes titles. One provider's free limit over? It hops to the next automatically. No key? The engine still runs — signal-only, nothing breaks.

4

Post-ready pack

Every clip ships as a vertical MP4 — captions up high where thumbs can read them, face kept in frame, the creator's own voice front and centre (no background music drowning it) — plus a ready description with source credit and hashtags. Post it as-is.

03

Built for how India actually streams

Hinglish captions, done properly

Auto-captions mangle Hinglish — research puts plain transcription error near 47% on code-mixed speech. Our correction pass fixes misheard words and writes Roman Hinglish the way your audience reads it. “Bhai” stays “bhai”, never “brother”.

Captions where eyes are

2–3 words at a time, bold, boxed, placed up high — clear of the platform UI at the bottom. Most viewers watch on mute; captions are not a feature here, they are the product.

Faces stay in frame

Podcast mode tracks faces shot by shot and stacks both speakers when a two-shot moment hits. Gaming mode keeps the action centred with a punch zoom on the peak second. Full-frame vertical — never a blurry padded band.

Bring-your-own-AI, zero markup

The AI layer runs on your own free-tier keys with an automatic fallback chain — Groq runs out, Gemini takes over, OpenRouter after that. We never sell you AI tokens, and the engine works even with no key at all.

Credit is automatic

Every export pack carries the source credit in its description file — the original creator is named in every pack, every time. No background music is added; the moment sounds like the moment.

Made to be posted

9:16 MP4s sized for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, paced at a snappy 1.08×, with a suggested title, hook line and hashtags written for each clip.

Straight answers

Is the web app live right now?

The clipping engine is built and running — the demo page shows its real output from a real video. We are onboarding the first cohort from the waitlist and clipping their first VODs directly while the self-serve web app is finished. Join the waitlist and your first VOD is on us.

Whose videos can I clip?

Your own streams and VODs, or content you have clear permission to use. RedCap is rights-first: campaigns and clips run on content the creator owns or has licensed. We do not clip other creators' work for resale.

What about copyright?

Every clip pack includes the source credit in its description. Gameplay clipping is common practice, but the rights to any video you submit stay yours to hold — if you don't have the rights, don't clip it.

How does the moment score work?

Five signals — speech (30), audio (25), motion (20), facecam (15), chat (10) — scored per moment and shown as a breakdown, exactly like the real run above. A moment must fire on at least 3 signals to qualify. If a signal has no data (a VOD has no live chat, say), the score says so instead of faking it.

What does the AI cost me?

Nothing extra. The AI pass uses your own free-tier key from Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, chained so one limit ending never stops a run. Prefer no AI at all? The engine's signal-only mode still finds and cuts your clips.

Which platforms can I post to?

Exports are 9:16 MP4s made for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok, with per-clip titles and hashtags in the pack.
